# Pacing Loans Against Traffic Baselines via Steganographic Bulletin Boards for Transport Fingerprinting

## The Idea
This proposal targets availability under an adaptive censor while protecting benign users in the **censorship_resistance** domain. It combines a
steganographic bulletin boards — where messages hidden inside high-volume media comment streams — with a deliberately adversarial
reading of the transport fingerprinting problem: statistical classification separates circumvention traffic from benign flows. The novel step is the
twist: bursts borrow from measured regional baselines, then repay with quiet periods.

In operation, the system treats collateral cost as the first-class observable. Every
design decision is evaluated against it under the model of an adaptive censor running active probes and statistical classifiers. Unlike a
straight deployment of steganographic bulletin boards, the twist changes what an observer can learn
from the outside: the mechanism's cost curve is reshaped so that the promised improvement
holds where it matters — no client-side code installs.

Why now: the channel window shows sustained interest in covert_channel, pluggable_transport-adjacent
techniques, while the open problem remains transport fingerprinting. The combination is new in
this framing, and its falsifiable form makes it testable with a prototype plus simulation
before any large-scale commitment.

## Hypothesis
We hypothesize that bursts borrow from measured regional baselines, then repay with quiet periods — implemented through messages hidden inside high-volume media comment streams — can hold classifier false-negative rates at ninety-five percent or higher when addressing statistical classification separates circumvention traffic from benign flows, under the constraint of no client-side code installs, measured by collateral cost.

## Proposed Testing Method
Build a minimal prototype of steganographic bulletin boards with pacing loans against traffic baselines and evaluate against a baseline without the twist. Simulation first: model an adaptive censor running active probes and statistical classifiers and replay traces sampled from the domain's channel window plus synthetic workloads. Vary one factor at a time; report reachability under probing, false-negative rate, collateral cost with confidence intervals over at least thirty seeds. Acceptance: the hypothesis improvement must hold at p<0.05 with the twist alone, and regressions on the other metrics must stay within ten percent of baseline. Follow with a small live-network pilot if simulation gates pass.
